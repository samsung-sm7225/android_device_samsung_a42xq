Device configuration for Samsung Galaxy A42/M42 5G
===============================================
The Samsung Galaxy A42/M42 5G (codename a42xq) is a upper mid-range smartphone from Samsung.

It was announced in September 2020 and released in November 2020.

There are also other different variants: 

* International (SM-A426B)
* India (SM-M426B)
* Korea (SM-A426N)
* America (SM-A426U)
* Hong-Kong (SM-A4260)

## Device specifications

Basic    | Spec Sheet
--------:|:----------------------
SoC      | Qualcomm Snapdragon 750G
CPU      | Octa-core (6x1.8ghz Kryo 570 \& 2x2.2ghz Cortex A77)
GPU      | Adreno 619
Memory   | 4,6,8GB RAM
Storage  | 128GB UFS
Expansion | Expandable up to 1TB via microSD
Shipped Android version | Android 10, OneUI 2
Battery  | Li-ion 5000mAh, non-removable, 25W Wired
Display  | Super AMOLED, 60Hz, 6.6 inches, 720 x 1600 pixels, 20:9 ratio
Camera Back  | 48 MP f/1.8 (wide), 8 MP f/2.2 123˚ (ultrawide), 5 MP f/2.4 (macro)
Camera Front | 	20 MP f/2.2 (wide)
Video        | 4K@30fps, LED Flash
Biometrics   | Face Recognition \& Fingerprint Sensor (under display, optical)
Sensors      | Gyroscope, Compass, Proximity Sensor, Accelerometer
